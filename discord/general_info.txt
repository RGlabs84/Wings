# 📜 WINGS OF THE VALKYRIE: THE SKALD's GUIDE 📜

Hail, Einherjar! Welcome to **Wings of the Valkyrie**. 
The gods have granted you dominion over the skies. This scroll contains all the sacred knowledge on how to craft your wings, master the winds, and configure the magic.

---

### 🕹️ MASTERING THE WINDS (FLIGHT CONTROLS)
- **Taking Flight:** Leap from any cliff or jump into the air, and press `Jump` a second time to unfurl your wings. The magic will catch the wind, entering an **Auto-Glide**. 
- **Flapping (Ascension):** Press `Jump` while gliding to thrust your wings downwards, granting a burst of vertical lift. Be warned: the Allfather demands energy, and every flap consumes Stamina. If your stamina depletes, you can no longer climb!
- **Diving:** While gliding, look down towards the earth to angle your wings and rapidly accelerate your descent. Perfect for escaping drakes or dropping into a fight!
- **Odin's Protection (No Fall Damage):** As long as your wings are actively deployed and glowing, you are immune to the shattering bones of a long fall.
- **The Flight Ceiling:** You cannot breach the realm of Asgard! Each wing tier has a maximum altitude above the ground. If you fly too high, the air grows thin and flapping will no longer grant upward lift until you descend.

---

### 🛠️ THE WING TIERS & CRAFTING
*(Note: All stats below are the default values written in the ancient runes. Server admins can rewrite these via the config file!)*

🟢 **Tier 1: Crude Wings**
*Tied together with basic hides, these are your first step into the sky. Low ceiling and slow glide.*
- **Forge:** Workbench (Level 1) | 10x Feathers, 10x Leather Scraps
- **Glide Speed:** 10 | **Flap Lift:** 15 | **Flight Ceiling:** 120m
- **Stamina Tribute:** 10 per flap

🔵 **Tier 2: Troll Hide Wings**
*Woven from the thick blue hide of trolls, granting improved aerodynamics and a lighter stamina burden.*
- **Forge:** Workbench (Level 2) | 5x Troll Hide, 15x Feathers
- **Glide Speed:** 15 | **Flap Lift:** 18 | **Flight Ceiling:** 135m
- **Stamina Tribute:** 8 per flap

🟣 **Tier 3: Lox Feathered Cloak**
*Heavy, powerful wings reinforced with silver. Extremely fast glide and massive lift capable of carrying a fully armored warrior.*
- **Forge:** Forge (Level 1) | 5x Lox Pelt, 5x Silver
- **Glide Speed:** 20 | **Flap Lift:** 22 | **Flight Ceiling:** 160m
- **Stamina Tribute:** 6 per flap

🟠 **Tier 4: Dragon Valkyrie Wings**
*The ultimate mastery of the skies. Woven with pure magical Eitr, these god-tier wings grant dominion over the Tenth World.*
- **Forge:** Galdr Table (Level 1) | 20x Feathers, 5x Eitr
- **Glide Speed:** 30 | **Flap Lift:** 28 | **Flight Ceiling:** 1100m *(Reach the World Tree!)*
- **Stamina Tribute:** 4 per flap

### ⚙️ THE RUNES OF CONFIGURATION (MULTIPLAYER)
- **ServerSync is absolute!** If you are running this mod on a dedicated server, install it on both the server and the clients. 
- Only Server Admins possess the authority to modify the configuration file (`BepInEx/config/wubarrk.wingsofthevalkyrie.cfg`).
- Changing the config on the server will automatically lock and sync the settings to all connected warriors instantly. No relogging required.
- You can completely customize crafting requirements, stamina costs, speeds, and forces to balance the skies perfectly for your server's saga.
