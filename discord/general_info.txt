# 📜 WINGS OF THE VALKYRIE: THE SKALD's GUIDE 📜

Hail, Einherjar! Welcome to **Wings of the Valkyrie**. 
The gods have granted you dominion over the skies. This scroll contains all the sacred knowledge on how to craft your wings, master the winds, and configure the magic.

---

### 🕹️ MASTERING THE WINDS (FLIGHT CONTROLS)
- **Taking Flight:** Leap from any cliff or jump into the air, and press `Jump` a second time to unfurl your wings. The magic will catch the wind, entering an **Auto-Glide**. 
- **Flapping (Ascension):** Press `Jump` while gliding to thrust your wings downwards, granting a burst of vertical lift. Be warned: the Allfather demands energy, and every flap consumes Stamina. If your stamina depletes, you can no longer climb!
- **Earning the Beat:** The greater wings will not answer a beginner. Troll, Lox and Dragon wings each demand a minimum **Valkyrie Flight** level before they will flap at all (15, 30 and 50 by default). They will always unfurl and always glide — and gliding is what earns the skill — so you can never be stranded, only humbled.
- **Diving:** While gliding, look down towards the earth to angle your wings and rapidly accelerate your descent. Perfect for escaping drakes or dropping into a fight!
- **Odin's Protection (No Fall Damage):** As long as your wings are actively deployed and glowing, you are immune to the shattering bones of a long fall.
- **The Flight Ceiling:** You cannot breach the realm of Asgard! Each wing tier has a maximum altitude above the ground. If you fly too high, the air grows thin and flapping will no longer grant upward lift until you descend.
- **The Ceiling Is Earned:** As of 2.0.1 that ceiling belongs to your *skill*, not merely your wings. The height listed for each tier is what a master at **Valkyrie Flight 100** reaches; at level 0 you claim only about a third of it, and the rest rises with you as you learn. The same Dragon wings that carry a veteran to 1300m will hold a newcomer near 455m.

---

### ⚔️ THE SKILL: VALKYRIE FLIGHT
Every wingbeat and every second on the wind raises a true Valheim skill, sitting in your skills panel alongside Running and Swords. At level 100 a master flaps for 45% of the stamina, strikes the air with +50% lift, sinks 60% slower on a level glide, glides 35% faster, and unlocks the full flight ceiling of whatever wings they wear. At level 0, they have none of that.

Diving is the one thing skill never touches — pointing yourself at the ground is intent, not practice. And death docks the skill like any other; the Valkyries respect only the living.

### 📖 THE FLIGHT LOGBOOK
Your character keeps its own saga of the sky. Open the console and type:
- `wov log` — time flown, distance, flights, wingbeats, and your records: highest altitude, longest flight, top speed, steepest stoop
- `wov oddities` — the stranger corners: hours flown by night, times you ran dry mid-air, times the wings refused you, where you tend to land, biomes crossed, and time spent in each tier
- `wov export` — writes the server's flight statistics file out now
- `wov where` — where that file lives

The logbook rides in your character save, so it follows you across worlds and servers. Every Viking keeps their own. Set `EnableFlightLog` to false if you would rather go uncounted.

**Asking BarrkBOT.** Everything the logbook measures happens on YOUR machine, so your game sends its totals up to the server roughly once a minute, and the server gathers everyone's into one file. That is what BarrkBOT reads when you ask him how long you have flown, how far, or how high. Set `PublishFlightStats` to false if you want your logbook kept private — you lose nothing but your place in the server's tally. Everyone needs 2.0.1 for this: a 2.0.0 client never reports and simply will not appear.

### 🛠️ THE WING TIERS & CRAFTING
*(Note: All stats below are default values. Server admins can customize recipes and stats via the config file! Stats are shown as novice → master, because every one of them now depends on your Valkyrie Flight level.)*

*Hides of the lowlands stretched over a bronze-nailed frame. Your first honest step into the sky.*

### ⚙️ THE RUNES OF CONFIGURATION (MULTIPLAYER)
- **ServerSync is absolute!** If you are running this mod on a dedicated server, install it on both the server and the clients. 
- Only Server Admins possess the authority to modify the configuration file (`BepInEx/config/wubarrk.wingsofthevalkyrie.cfg`).
- Changing the config on the server will automatically lock and sync the settings to all connected warriors instantly. No relogging required.
- You can completely customize crafting requirements, stamina costs, speeds, forces, skill requirements and the whole skill curve to balance the skies perfectly for your server's saga.
- Prefer the old 2.0.0 feel? Set `CeilingAtNovice` to 1 and every `MinSkillToFlap` to 0 — altitude and flapping stop being gated entirely.
- The `7. Flight Logbook` section is deliberately NOT server-synced. Whether your own flights are written down is your business, not the server's.
