The biggest update in the mod's history just landed. v2 turns flying from a thing you build into a thing you *master* — and the sky keeps score.

🪽 **NEW: THE VALKYRIE FLIGHT SKILL**
Every flap and every second on the wind levels a true Valheim skill, right in your skills panel with its own icon. Veterans of the sky flap for **half the stamina**, strike with **+30% more lift**, sink **half as slowly** on the glide, and cut the wind **faster**. Die, and the Valkyries take their tithe — just like any skill.

No more bird-scaring your way into the clouds with a handful of feathers. Crude Wings are now a proper Bronze Age project — bronze nails and a mix of Meadows and Black Forest hides — and every tier above costs what dominion over the sky *should*: iron nails, silver and linen, and for the Dragon Wings... the tears of a dragon herself.

🎨 **HAND-FORGED ICONS**
All four wing tiers and the skill carry their own crafted sigils in your inventory. No more borrowed cape art.
